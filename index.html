QUESTIONS WE HEAR A LOT

Frequently asked.

Why bundle everything instead of just building a website?

A beautiful website with no SEO doesn't get found. A site with no content looks generic. Ad spend with no funnel underneath leaks money. The four pillars work because they're integrated — that's why we bundle them. If your budget or timeline only allows one piece, we'll scope it à la carte.

What platforms do you build websites on?

We meet you where you already are. Most projects land on Square, Squarespace, Webflow, WordPress, or Wix. We'll recommend the right platform based on your business, your team's tech comfort, and what your booking, ordering, or contact flow actually needs to do.

Do I own the website and content?

Yes. Every deliverable we create — website files, photos, video, ad creative, keyword research, performance reports — is yours, perpetually, no strings. That's true whether you stay on with us for one month or one year. We retain the right to feature the work in our portfolio.

What is AI SEO and why should I care?

People aren't just typing "best gym near me" into Google anymore — they're asking ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews for recommendations directly. These answer engines pull from a different set of signals than classic Google rank: schema markup, structured content, citations on review sites, and clean conversational copy on your pages. Most local businesses (and most agencies) aren't optimizing for any of this yet. We bake AI search visibility into every project so you're showing up where the next wave of customers is actually looking.

How are your ads different from what an ad agency would run?

Most agencies running local ads do three things wrong: weak audience targeting (interest-based "spray and pray" instead of geographic + behavior), recycled creative that doesn't match the business, and zero conversion tracking so they can claim success without proving ROI. We build campaigns around your real customers, use creative from your own content shoot, and install full conversion tracking from day one — so you actually know what's working.

How does monthly billing actually work?

Flat monthly rate, scoped on a discovery call based on which pillars you want active. That rate covers everything in your agreed scope — no overage invoices, no surprise charges. Most clients start with the full bundle for the first few months to ship the foundational work (website, shoot, SEO, ad setup), then scale to whichever pillars they want ongoing. You can add, drop, or pause pillars month to month, and cancel with 30 days notice. No long-term lock-in.

What if I don't like the website?

Revision rounds are built into the scope at each major milestone. We approve the design direction before we build, and the build before we go live. You won't be surprised. Unlimited "change everything" rounds aren't included — that's how projects die — but the planned rounds are more than enough when we get the brief right.
