How Denver & Boulder businesses show up in ChatGPT & Google AI Overviews

The way people search is splitting in two. Some still type "best gym near me" into Google. A fast-growing share now just ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or Google's AI Overviews: "What's a good gym in Denver with childcare?" — and take the single answer the AI gives them. If your business isn't the one it names, you never enter the conversation.

For local businesses in Denver, Boulder, and along the Front Range, this is a rare early-mover moment. Most competitors haven't optimized for AI search at all yet, so the bar to show up is still low.

Why AI engines recommend one business over another

Answer engines don't crawl the web the way classic Google rank does. They lean on a different stack of signals:

  • Structured data (schema): machine-readable markup that tells an AI exactly what you do, where you serve, your services, hours, and FAQs.
  • Clear, conversational copy: pages written the way people actually ask questions, so the model can lift a clean answer.
  • Google Business Profile: a complete, active profile is one of the strongest local signals AI leans on.
  • Reviews and citations: consistent mentions across the web and real reviews build the trust these models weight heavily.

What to do this quarter

  1. Add LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQ schema to your site so AI can parse your business.
  2. Rewrite key pages around the real questions your customers ask ("how much," "how long," "do you serve my area").
  3. Complete and actively post to your Google Business Profile with your Denver/Boulder service area.
  4. Get consistent citations (name, address, phone) and a steady flow of reviews.
Illustrative example

A hypothetical Boulder pilates studio adds service + FAQ schema, cleans up its Google Business Profile, and answers ten common client questions in plain language on its site. Within a couple of months, "pilates studio in Boulder with reformer classes" style questions start surfacing its name in AI answers — the kind of result this approach is designed to produce.

None of this is a trick. It's making your business legible to the systems people now ask for recommendations. Do it before your competitors do and you own the answer.

Frequently asked

What is AI search optimization (AEO)?

It's optimizing your website and business data so AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews, and Gemini — recommend you when someone asks for a business like yours. It relies on clean structured data (schema), clear conversational copy, and strong signals like reviews and citations.

How is this different from normal SEO?

Traditional SEO targets the ranked blue links on Google. AI search pulls from a different mix of signals to generate a single spoken or written recommendation. You want both: classic ranking plus being the name the AI names.

Can a Denver small business actually show up in ChatGPT?

Yes. Local businesses with well-structured websites, a complete Google Business Profile, consistent citations, and genuine reviews are already being surfaced in AI answers for 'near me' style questions across Denver and Boulder.
