How to rank in the Denver map pack

The three levers Google weighs for local ranking — and the profile checklist that gets you into the map pack.

Home / Insights / How to rank in the Denver map pack
ace signal media · Denver & Boulder · Updated Aug 2026

For any Denver or Boulder business that people search for locally, the Google map pack — those top three map results — is prime real estate. It often gets more clicks than the #1 blue link. Here's how to earn a spot.

The three levers Google actually weighs

  • Proximity: how close you are to the searcher. You can't move your shop, but you can define an accurate service area.
  • Relevance: how well your profile matches the search — driven by categories, services, and description.
  • Prominence: how established you look — reviews, citations, posts, and website authority.

Your Google Business Profile checklist

  1. Fill out every field — primary + secondary categories, services, hours, attributes, and a keyword-aware description.
  2. Keep your name, address, and phone identical everywhere online (this consistency is called citations).
  3. Add real photos regularly — profiles with fresh photos convert far better.
  4. Post updates weekly (offers, news, projects) — an active profile ranks better than a dormant one.
  5. Ask every happy customer for a review, and reply to all of them.
Illustrative example

Picture a hypothetical Lafayette dental practice that fixes its categories, adds weekly posts, and asks each patient for a review. As reviews and activity build, it climbs into the local three-pack for "dentist near me" in its area — exactly the trajectory a disciplined profile is built to create.

The Google Business Profile is free, and it's the single highest-leverage local SEO asset you own. The schema and content on your website reinforce it — together they're what get you into the map pack and into AI recommendations.

Frequently asked

How do I rank higher in the Google map pack in Denver?

Complete every field of your Google Business Profile, pick precise categories, keep your name/address/phone consistent everywhere, post regularly, add photos, and earn steady reviews. Proximity, relevance, and prominence are the three levers Google weighs.

How important are reviews for local ranking?

Very. Review quantity, recency, rating, and even the keywords customers use in reviews all feed local ranking — and they heavily influence whether AI engines recommend you too.

How long until my Google Business Profile ranks?

Basic improvements can move you within weeks; competitive Denver and Boulder categories take a few months of consistent posting, reviews, and citations to climb.
