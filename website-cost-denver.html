What does a small business website cost in Denver?

A straight, no-jargon breakdown of what a website really costs in 2026 — and what makes one actually generate bookings.

Home / Insights / What does a small business website cost in Denver?
ace signal media · Denver & Boulder · Updated Aug 2026

"What does a website cost?" is the first question almost every Denver and Boulder business owner asks — and the honest answer is: it depends on what the site needs to do. Here's a straight, no-jargon breakdown so you can budget with confidence.

The realistic ranges

  • DIY templates (Wix, Squarespace): a few hundred dollars a year. Fine to start, but you're the designer, copywriter, and SEO.
  • Freelancer: low four figures. Quality swings hard depending on who you hire.
  • Professional mobile-first build: mid four figures for most local businesses — conversion-focused layout, real copy, local SEO, and QA.
  • Flat monthly (bundled): a predictable monthly rate that rolls the site together with content, ads, and search visibility, with no surprise invoices.

What actually drives the price

Number of pages, whether you need booking or online ordering, how much copy and photography is produced fresh, and how much SEO and conversion tracking is built in. The cheapest site is rarely the cheapest outcome — a site that doesn't get found or doesn't convert costs you customers every week.

Illustrative example

Consider a hypothetical Denver barbershop weighing a $600 DIY site against a professional rebuild. The DIY site looks fine but buries the booking button and never ranks. The rebuild puts booking front-and-center, loads fast on phones, and targets "barber near me" locally — the difference isn't the build cost, it's the chairs filled every week afterward.

Questions to ask before you pay anyone

  1. Is it mobile-first, and how fast does it load on a phone?
  2. Is the main action (book/order/call) obvious without scrolling?
  3. Is local SEO and schema included so Denver/Boulder customers find you?
  4. Is conversion tracking installed so you can prove ROI?
  5. Do I own the site and content if we part ways?

Frequently asked

How much does a small business website cost in Denver?

It ranges widely — from a few hundred dollars for a DIY template to five figures for a large custom build. Most Denver and Boulder small businesses land in the low-to-mid four figures for a professional, mobile-first site, or a flat monthly rate that bundles the site with content, ads, and SEO.

Should I pay monthly or one-time for a website?

Both models exist. A one-time build is a fixed project fee. A flat monthly model spreads the cost and usually bundles ongoing updates, content, and marketing so the site keeps improving instead of going stale.

What makes a website actually generate bookings?

Fast mobile load, an obvious call-to-action (book/order/call) above the fold, clear local SEO so you get found, and conversion tracking so you know what's working. A pretty site with none of these rarely books anyone.
